# Summary Report: **FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week: Show Up. Build. Pitch. WIN!**

### Event Objectives

- Introduce participants to the concept of **Agentic AI** and its applications in modern software development.
- Demonstrate how AI-powered agents can accelerate the software development lifecycle.
- Encourage participants to build practical AI solutions through hands-on development and teamwork.

### Speakers

- **AWS Community Builders and FCAJ Organizers**
- Industry experts and mentors in AI, cloud computing, and software engineering.

### Key Highlights

#### Introduction to Agentic AI

- Overview of **Agentic AI** and how autonomous AI agents differ from traditional AI assistants.
- The role of AI agents in planning, reasoning, tool usage, and decision-making.
- Real-world applications of Agentic AI across software development and business automation.

### Key Takeaways

#### Understanding Agentic AI

- Agentic AI extends beyond traditional chatbots by enabling autonomous planning and execution.
- AI agents can interact with external tools and services to complete complex workflows.
- Selecting the appropriate AI architecture depends on project requirements and expected outcomes.

#### Software Development

- AI can significantly improve developer productivity by assisting with coding, debugging, testing, and documentation.
- Human oversight remains essential to validate AI-generated solutions and maintain software quality.
- Effective collaboration between developers and AI leads to faster and more efficient development.

#### Project Development

- Successful AI applications require clear problem definition before implementation.
- Iterative development and continuous testing improve project quality.
- Well-prepared demonstrations and presentations are important for communicating technical ideas effectively.
